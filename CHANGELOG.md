# brag-motion

## 0.1.0

### Initial release

- Carried-element scene changes: one element of the outgoing scene transforms while the next scene assembles around it.
- Timing profile: 0.8–1.0 s travel with strong in-out easing; exits accelerate, entrances decelerate.
- Length computed bottom-up from the product flow, with at least 2 s of look per screen.
- Change QA with snapshots at 0/25/50/75/100 % of every change, including content-swap legibility.
- Plugin and marketplace manifests, evals.
